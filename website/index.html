Dark is fine.

The front desk keeps checking people in, taking payments and logging every punch. When the line comes back, it all syncs in batches. Nothing’s lost. Nothing’s counted twice.

Signed, not stored.

Access runs on time-bound leases signed in the cloud with a key that never leaves a hardware module. The gym PC only holds the public half. Edit the database all you like. It won’t open a door.

The turnstile remembers.

Expiry dates get written into the terminal’s own memory. Yank the Ethernet cable and the hardware still shuts the door on time.

Five checks on every scan.

Signature valid. Clock hasn’t rolled back. Gym lease alive. Member lease alive. Dues paid, or inside the deadline. Fail one and the scan’s rejected, logged, and flashed on the reception screen.

Exit’s always free.

Membership lapses while someone’s mid-workout? The exit gate opens. The entry reader just won’t let them back in. No one gets trapped inside.

Money splits & syncs via phone.

UPI Autopay and cards run through Razorpay Route. Payment fails while gym Wi-Fi is dead? The member’s phone gets a signed 48-hour grace lease over 5G and hands it to the offline gate via QR/NFC scan. No internet needed at the door.

Clock tricks fail.

Time is tracked in three places at once. If they disagree by ten minutes, the system locks and tells us.

No rip-and-replace.

Keep your existing ₹1.5L turnstiles. The BastionGate DIN-rail Edge Unit drops right in front of your eSSL, ZKTeco, or Hikvision relay wires in 20 minutes.

Helpdesk built in.

Receptionist logs a ticket offline. It queues, then ships with connection diagnostics attached.
